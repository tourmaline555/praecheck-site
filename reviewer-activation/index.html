Google Play reviewer activation

This page is for authorised Google Play review of Praecheck. It issues a free, installation-bound review licence. No payment or purchase transaction is required.

After downloading the licence
  1. Return to Praecheck.
  2. Tap Import Praecheck licence.
  3. Select the downloaded .pcl file.
  4. The review licence unlocks the full app on that installation.

Praecheck itself verifies the signed licence offline. The production Android app does not require Android INTERNET permission for assessment or licence verification.
