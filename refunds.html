Refund Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Statutory rights

Nothing in this Refund Policy limits or excludes any mandatory consumer rights that apply to you, including rights relating to digital content that is faulty, not as described or otherwise does not conform to applicable law.

Refund requests

If you believe you are entitled to a refund, or you wish to ask us to consider a voluntary refund, contact Praescius Ltd at info@praescius.org.uk.

Please include the email address used for the purchase and, where available, your order or transaction reference. Do not send full payment-card details.

Digital delivery and cancellation

Praecheck is supplied as digital content. Where you expressly request immediate supply before the end of any statutory cancellation period and acknowledge that your right to cancel will be lost once supply begins, that statutory cancellation right may no longer apply after delivery has started.

This does not affect any mandatory rights you may have where the digital content is faulty, not as described or otherwise does not conform to applicable law.

Payment provider and refund processing

Payments and refunds may be processed through a third-party payment provider or app marketplace. Where applicable, a refund may be returned through the original payment method or may need to be requested through the platform through which the purchase was made.

Refund processing times can depend on the payment provider, marketplace, bank or card issuer. Praecheck does not impose additional conditions that override your mandatory consumer rights.

Product support

If you experience a delivery, activation or product issue, you can also contact Praecheck support so we can help resolve it.
