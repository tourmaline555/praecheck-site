Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy explains how Praescius Ltd handles personal information in connection with the Praecheck website, purchase process, licence activation and Android application.

1. Praecheck assessment data

Praecheck is designed to perform its handset assessment locally. The production Android application does not request Android's INTERNET permission or the Google advertising ID (AD_ID) permission. Installed-app context, assessment results, comparison history, device-role labels, recheck preferences and user-verification records are stored locally and are not uploaded as part of the assessment process.

Purchase and licence activation are separate from the assessment. To issue a licence, the Praecheck licensing service processes the purchase email, Stripe Checkout reference and Praecheck Installation Code submitted during activation. It does not receive your assessment findings or assessment history.

2. Website data

We do not intentionally use advertising trackers or analytics cookies on this website. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process routine technical information such as IP addresses, request timestamps and browser details for security and service operation.

3. Purchases and payment

Current Praecheck Personal web purchases are processed using Stripe Managed Payments. For Managed Payments transactions, Stripe (through Link) acts as merchant of record and may process payment, billing, tax, fraud, transaction and customer-support information under its own terms and privacy notices.

We receive transaction information needed to fulfil the purchase, issue and manage a Praecheck licence, provide product support, and handle refunds or disputes where appropriate. Payment-card details are handled by Stripe rather than by the Praecheck Android application.

4. Support communications

If you contact us, we process the information you provide so that we can respond, troubleshoot issues and keep a reasonable record of correspondence.

5. Legal bases and retention

Where applicable, we process purchase and support information to perform our contract with you, comply with legal obligations and pursue legitimate interests such as fraud prevention, accounting and customer support. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes or as required by law.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with service providers used to operate the website, process purchases, fulfil licences, provide support or comply with law.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to certain processing, and to request a copy of personal information we hold about you. Statutory rights vary by jurisdiction.
