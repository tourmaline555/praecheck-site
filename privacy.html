This policy explains how Praescius Ltd handles personal information in connection with the Praecheck website, purchase process and Android application.

1. Praecheck assessment data

Praecheck is designed to perform its handset assessment locally. The Android application does not upload assessment results to Praescius Ltd, and the application does not request Android's INTERNET permission.

2. Website data

We do not intentionally use advertising trackers or analytics cookies on this website. The site may be hosted by a third-party hosting provider, which may process routine technical information such as IP addresses, request timestamps and browser details for security and service operation.

3. Purchases and payment

Payments are processed by Paddle acting as merchant of record. Paddle may collect payment, billing, tax and transaction information under its own privacy terms. We may receive transaction information necessary to fulfil your purchase, provide support, issue a licence and manage refunds or disputes.

4. Support communications

If you contact us, we process the information you provide so that we can respond, troubleshoot issues and keep a reasonable record of correspondence.

5. Legal bases and retention

Where applicable, we process purchase and support information to perform our contract with you, comply with legal obligations and pursue legitimate interests such as fraud prevention, accounting and customer support. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes or as required by law.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with service providers used to operate the website, process purchases, fulfil licences, provide support or comply with law.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to certain processing, and to request a copy of personal information we hold about you. Statutory rights vary by jurisdiction.
