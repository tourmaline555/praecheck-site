PRAESCIUS / PRODUCTS

Know your phone.
Know your space.

Two Android products with different purposes. Praecheck helps you understand what your phone exposes. PraeGuard helps you monitor activity in a room, vehicle or other space when you're away.

ANDROID PRIVACY

Praecheck

Review privacy and data-exposure findings on your Android phone, see which apps are involved when attribution is supported, and compare results over time.

An on-device assessment — not a monitoring or intrusion-detection app.

Explore Praecheck ↗View on Google Play ↗

SPACE ACTIVITY MONITORING

PraeGuard

Leave a spare Android phone in a hotel room, parked vehicle or another space you're authorised to monitor. Arm the app before leaving; later review notable sounds and phone-handling activity. Optional Pro Gmail alerts can send notifications when connectivity permits.

A local activity record to help you investigate concerns, not proof that someone entered or acted improperly.

Available to enrolled Google Play internal testers; public release pending.

Explore PraeGuard ↗Google Play (testers) ↗

Praecheck and PraeGuard are separate products. Praecheck purchases and licences do not unlock PraeGuard Pro. Follow each product's own setup, privacy and usage guidance.
