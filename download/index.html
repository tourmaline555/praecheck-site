Install and activate

Three steps to get started.

The APK can be downloaded before or after purchase. A valid Praecheck licence file is still required for licensed use.

1

Install

Download the APK and open it on your Android device. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser or file manager.

2

Purchase

Choose Personal for up to 2 devices or Power for up to 10 personal devices. Secure payment is processed by Paddle.

3

Activate

Open Praecheck, copy the Installation ID, then use the activation page with your purchase reference to download the signed offline .pcl licence.

What's new in v0.9.3

Praecheck now helps you track change over time.

Assessment history

Keep a bounded local timeline of compatible assessments.

Changes and regressions

See new, resolved and returned findings between compatible assessments.

Recheck planning

Choose 30, 60 or 90-day reassessment intervals and reassess sooner after meaningful changes.

Device roles

Add Everyday, Banking, Work, Travel, Privacy-focused or Custom context without silently changing the Core score.

Version-aware comparisons

A changed assessment model starts a fresh comparison baseline rather than producing misleading regressions.
