PraeGuard terms of use

Draft for pre-release review · 5 November 2026

Pre-release draft: These terms are published for product transparency. They should receive legal review before commercial launch and may be updated when PraeGuard is listed on Google Play.

Authorised use only

You must only monitor premises and devices where you have appropriate authority and comply with local laws and rules about recording, privacy, notice and consent. Do not use PraeGuard to secretly monitor another person or to bypass Android privacy controls.

What the app provides

PraeGuard records available audio, motion and optional photo evidence and presents it for user review. Automated event descriptions and sound classifications may be mistaken or incomplete. The app does not verify someone's identity, prove entry, prevent access or guarantee a successful recording.

Device and network limitations

Android may restrict microphone, camera, Bluetooth or background activity. Monitoring depends on permissions, battery, storage, device hardware and platform behaviour. Remote alerts require compatible connectivity and Google authorisation and may be delayed or fail.

Free and Pro

Core features may be offered free, with additional features available through a one-time Google Play in-app purchase. Features, eligibility, pricing and refunds will be described in the app and Google Play listing. Purchases are subject to Google Play's applicable terms.

Evidence responsibility

You control which evidence to retain, export and share. Review labels exclude incidents from selected exports but do not erase the original device records. Back up important evidence before deleting local files or uninstalling. You are responsible for appropriate handling of sensitive recordings.

Support and contact

For support contact info@praescius.org.uk. Visit the PraeGuard privacy notice for information about personal-data processing.
