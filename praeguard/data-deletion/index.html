PraeGuard data deletion

How to remove evidence from your device, and how to ask Praescius Ltd to delete personal information it holds about you.

No PraeGuard account is required. PraeGuard does not currently operate a Praescius cloud evidence-storage service. Most monitoring evidence stays on your phone unless you choose to export or send it.

1. Delete recordings and incidents on your phone

  1. Open PraeGuard and disarm monitoring.
  2. Open Settings and select DELETE ALL SAVED EVIDENCE.
  3. Read the confirmation carefully. Export any evidence you wish to keep first, then confirm deletion if you want to proceed.
  4. For older saved audio and photos, the Settings → Storage → Manage storage controls also let you review and delete old files you select.

These controls are intended for locally saved monitoring evidence and queued alert material. You can also clear PraeGuard's app storage through Android settings, but doing so resets app data and preferences. Deletion from the app cannot remove copies you exported to another folder, cloud service or device.

Marking an incident Ignored or Marked as mine is not deletion; it changes its review/export status only.

2. Request deletion of data held by Praescius

If you contacted Praescius for support, made a commercial enquiry or otherwise supplied personal information to us, you can request deletion of eligible information we hold.

  1. Email info@praescius.org.uk with the subject PraeGuard data deletion request.
  2. Tell us what information you want deleted (for example, a support enquiry) and provide enough information for us to locate it, such as the email address you used to contact us.
  3. We will review the request, make any necessary identity checks proportionate to the data involved, and explain the outcome and any information we must retain.

Email a deletion request

You do not need to send recordings, photographs or identity documents with your initial request. This link opens your email app; no website form or automated deletion service is provided.

3. What can and cannot be deleted

  • On-device evidence: Recordings, incident information, review selections and related local files are controlled through the app and your Android device. Praescius cannot remotely erase files stored only on your phone.
  • Information sent to Praescius: We can assess deletion requests for personal information we hold through support or business communications, subject to legal obligations.
  • Gmail alerts: If you enabled optional alerts, messages and attachments already sent may remain in the sending account's Sent folder, the recipient's mailbox and email-provider systems. Deleting them on your phone will not recall them. Manage those copies with the relevant account or recipient.
  • Google Play purchases: Google handles purchase transactions and associated account records. For information held by Google, use Google's own privacy and account controls. Your entitlement may still need to be retained to support purchases.
  • Exported copies: Files saved or shared outside PraeGuard must be removed wherever you stored or shared them.

4. Retention and exceptions

PraeGuard does not automatically erase all local evidence after a fixed number of days; local data remains until you remove it or app/device actions remove it. The optional old-file cleanup is user initiated.

For personal data Praescius holds, we assess deletion requests under applicable data-protection law. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate legal claims or accounting obligations. If so, we will explain the category and reason when responding to your request. We do not promise a universal 90-day deletion period for every category of data.
