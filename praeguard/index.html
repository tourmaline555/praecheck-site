Why you might use it

You can't always be there. Your phone can keep a record.

Turn an Android phone you control into a local activity monitor. A useful option when you want to check what happened in a space while you were absent, without installing a permanent security system.

HOTEL ROOM / TRAVEL

You're out for dinner. What happened in your room?

You're travelling and concerned about unexpected access to your hotel room while you're away. Where hotel rules and recording laws permit, you can leave a spare Android phone in the room and arm PraeGuard before heading out.

  1. Place it: Check permissions and power, position your phone, and select the monitoring options you need.
  2. Arm and leave: PraeGuard runs locally and can register relevant sounds and handling of the device. With Pro enabled, selected events may trigger Gmail alerts when online.
  3. Review: On your return, disarm the app, inspect the incident timeline, play available evidence and choose which events to retain.

Potentially relevant observations are evidence to consider, not a verdict that someone entered or snooped.

PARKED VEHICLE

Keep a record while your vehicle is unattended.

Place your phone securely in a parked vehicle you control to review notable sounds or movement of the phone. Use only where recording is permitted, and take care with battery life and temperature.

OTHER AUTHORISED SPACES

Check activity while a space is empty.

Use it in an unoccupied room, workspace or storage area you have authority to monitor. Review the app's observations later, or use optional Pro alerts for selected events.

Important: PraeGuard analyses sounds in the phone's surroundings and events affecting the handset; it does not detect silent movement throughout a room, identify people, prove entry or prevent access. Use only with appropriate authorisation, notice and consent, and check local recording rules.

What PraeGuard does

Evidence you can follow. Not just sensor readings.

PraeGuard analyses nearby sounds and events affecting the phone itself, then organises relevant observations into an incident timeline. Camera evidence is optional and depends on Android device permissions and background restrictions.

The details

Questions worth asking.

Does PraeGuard upload recordings to Praescius?

No Praescius evidence-upload service is included in the current architecture. Monitoring and sound classification run on the device. Optional Gmail alerts transfer chosen files through your authorised Google account to your chosen recipient.

Will it work without internet?

Local detection and evidence review work offline. Remote Gmail alerts and purchase verification require a connection. Queued alerts depend on Android and Google account availability.

Can I exclude my own return to the room?

Yes. Mark an incident as yours or ignored. That removes it from the reviewed export while leaving the original local evidence available for reclassification.

Will PraeGuard tell me if somebody entered my hotel room?

Not with certainty. It can record relevant sounds and phone-handling events for you to review, but cannot detect silent room movement, identify people or prove entry. Optional Pro Gmail alerts depend on network access, your Google account and Android background behaviour.

Is this a hidden surveillance app?

No. PraeGuard is intended for transparent, user-initiated monitoring of spaces where recording is authorised. Android privacy indicators and monitoring notifications are not concealed.
