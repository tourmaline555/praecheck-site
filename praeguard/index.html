2

Leave the phone in position during the exit countdown and environmental baseline period.

Important limitation

Sound classification and motion sensing are indicators, not proof of unauthorised entry. Camera access can be limited while Android is locked, and background alerts may be delayed. Recording indicators remain visible.

The details

Questions worth asking.

Does PraeGuard upload recordings to Praescius?

No Praescius evidence-upload service is included in the current architecture. Monitoring and sound classification run on the device. Optional Gmail alerts transfer chosen files through your authorised Google account to your chosen recipient.

Will it work without internet?

Local detection and evidence review work offline. Remote Gmail alerts and purchase verification require a connection. Queued alerts depend on Android and Google account availability.

Can I exclude my own return to the room?

Yes. Mark an incident as yours or ignored. That removes it from the reviewed export while leaving the original local evidence available for reclassification.

Is this a hidden surveillance app?

No. PraeGuard is intended for transparent, user-initiated monitoring of spaces where recording is authorised. Android privacy indicators and monitoring notifications are not concealed.
