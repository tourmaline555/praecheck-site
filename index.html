Android data exposure assessment

Assess Android data exposure. Track change over time.

Praecheck performs a local assessment of Android configuration, granted permissions, connectivity and installed-app characteristics. Findings are prioritised with evidence context, and compatible assessments can be compared over time.

Core exposure and optional app-correlation analysis are kept separate so observed device state is not conflated with inferred correlation potential.

Assessment interface

Evidence, prioritisation and reassessment in one workflow.

Praecheck is structured around the assessment lifecycle: establish current exposure, distinguish observed state from items requiring verification, review evidence and remediation, then compare later assessments under a compatible model.

Prioritisation queue

Exposure and verification are handled separately.

The Actions view separates prioritised exposure findings from checks requiring user verification. Severity, score contribution and recommended action are presented together.

Finding evidence

Evidence and remediation remain explicit.

Finding detail records the rationale, evidence source, confidence and relevant remediation, including direct access to Android settings where supported.

App-correlation analysis

Inferred correlation is scored independently.

Optional static app analysis uses a distinct exposure index so inferred correlation surface remains separate from the Core assessment of observed or currently granted exposure.

Longitudinal history

Compatible assessments can be compared over time.

Local history records device role, app and assessment-model versions, Core and App scores, and new, returned, resolved and unchanged findings.

Privacy architecture

Assessment processing remains on the handset.

Production APK: no Android INTERNET permission

The production Android application performs its assessment locally and does not request Android's INTERNET permission. Assessment results, comparison history and role context are not uploaded to a Praecheck account or remote scanning service.

Praecheck is a data exposure assessment tool. It is not antivirus and does not claim to detect malware, spyware or compromise.

L

Local processing

Assessment logic executes on the handset against locally available Android state.

O

Offline assessment

The assessment does not depend on a cloud account, remote scan engine or network lookup.

I

Independent criteria

Assessment findings are not driven by advertising, affiliate placement or sponsored ranking.

Technical notes

Scope, scoring and licensing.

What is the assessment scope?

Praecheck reviews locally available Android settings, app permissions, connectivity and installed-app characteristics to identify areas that may increase personal data exposure or correlation potential. It then provides prioritised findings and practical actions.

Does the production app transmit assessment data?

No. The production Android app is designed to perform its assessment locally and does not request Android's INTERNET permission. Assessment results and history are stored on the device.

Is Praecheck a malware, spyware or compromise detector?

No. Praecheck is a data exposure assessment tool. It does not claim to detect malware, spyware, surveillance actors or compromise.

What do the Core and App scores mean?

Core prioritises current device exposure from supported checks. The optional App score represents correlation potential from installed-app characteristics and recognised indicators. Neither score is evidence that a phone has been compromised.

Why are repeated assessments useful?

Your exposure profile can change when you install or remove apps, change permissions, update Android, travel, change networks or use the device differently. Praecheck can compare compatible local assessments to show what changed.

How is assessment history retained?

Assessment history, device role and recheck preferences are stored locally on the Android device. Praecheck keeps a bounded history of up to 50 assessments.

How do Personal and Power licences differ?

Both include the same Praecheck features. Personal allows activation on up to 2 personal devices; Power allows activation on up to 10 personal devices. Power does not add business, consultant or resale rights.

How is Praecheck distributed and activated?

Download the signed APK from the official Praecheck download page, install it on Android, purchase a licence, then use the activation page with the Installation ID shown in Praecheck and your Paddle purchase reference to obtain the signed offline .pcl licence.
