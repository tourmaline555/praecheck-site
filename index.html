What Praecheck checks

A clearer view of what your phone exposes.

Praecheck separates observed device state, granted access, declared app capabilities and checks that Android restricts from ordinary apps.

01

Apps & permissions

Highlights sensitive granted permissions, background location, relationship-rich access and other high-value permission combinations.

02

Network & DNS

Reviews active VPN state, Private DNS behaviour and relevant network configuration without uploading scan results.

03

Bluetooth & nearby

Surfaces current bonded-device relationships and granted Nearby/Bluetooth access that can reveal association and context.

04

Installed-app characteristics

Identifies outdated target APIs, debuggable builds, declaration-only capabilities and other configuration signals.

05

App correlation analysis

Optionally assesses recognised SDK indicators and useful data combinations while keeping inferred capability separate from observed permission state.

06

Changes over time

Keep a local assessment history, compare compatible assessments, see new and resolved findings, flag findings that return, and export detailed results to CSV.

How it works

Assess. Act. Reassess. Compare.

Praecheck turns technical handset state into prioritised actions, then shows whether your exposure profile improved or changed.

1

Assess

Praecheck inspects locally available Android state and app metadata.

2

Act

Review prioritised findings and use practical recommendations to reduce access or configuration you no longer need.

3

Reassess

Run Praecheck again after permission changes, app installs, Android updates, travel or other meaningful changes.

4

Compare

See what is new, what is resolved, what remains, and whether a previously resolved finding has returned.

Continuous value

Your exposure profile does not stand still.

Installing apps, changing permissions, travelling, updating Android or changing how you use a device can alter its exposure profile. Praecheck keeps compatible assessment history locally so you can see what changed without sending assessment data off the device.

Δ

Changes since last assessment

See new, resolved and unchanged findings, with score movement when assessments use the same scoring model.

↻

Returned findings

Praecheck can highlight a finding that was previously resolved and later reappears, helping you spot regressions.

30

Recheck planning

Choose a local 30, 60 or 90-day recheck interval and reassess sooner after meaningful device, app, permission or connectivity changes.

H

Local assessment history

Keep a bounded on-device timeline of assessments with scores, finding counts, app version and assessment-model context.

R

Device roles

Tag assessments as Everyday, Banking, Work, Travel, Privacy-focused or Custom to add context without silently changing the Core score.

V

Version-aware reassessment

Praecheck records app and assessment-model versions so a changed check set starts a fresh comparison baseline instead of creating misleading regressions.

Local by design

Your assessment stays on your device.

Praecheck is designed to perform its assessment locally. The Android app does not upload your assessment results, and it does not request Android's INTERNET permission.

L

Local

Assessment logic runs on the handset using locally available Android state.

O

Offline

Core assessment does not depend on a cloud account or remote scan service.

I

Independent

Findings are based on device state and documented app characteristics rather than advertising or affiliate incentives.

FAQ

Useful things to know.

Is Praecheck antivirus?

No. Praecheck is a data exposure assessment tool. It does not claim to detect malware, spyware or surveillance actors.

Does Praecheck upload my scan?

No. Praecheck is designed to assess the handset locally and does not upload assessment results.

Why are some checks marked capability or verify?

Android limits what an ordinary app can prove. Praecheck distinguishes granted or observed state from app declarations, inferred capability and checks that require user verification.

Does a high score mean my phone is compromised?

No. Scores prioritise exposure and correlation potential. They are not evidence of compromise, collection by a specific party or threat-actor access.

Why would I run Praecheck again?

Your exposure profile can change when you install or remove apps, change permissions, update Android, travel, change networks or use the device for a different role. Praecheck can compare compatible local assessments so you can see what changed, what was resolved and what has returned.

Where is my assessment history stored?

Assessment history, device role and recheck preferences are stored locally on the Android device. Praecheck does not upload assessment results.

What is the difference between Personal and Power?

Both tiers include the same Praecheck assessment features. Personal allows activation on up to 2 personal devices; Power allows activation on up to 10 personal devices. Power does not add business, consultant or resale rights.

How is Praecheck delivered?

Praecheck is supplied as Android software with an offline licence file. Personal covers up to 2 devices and Power up to 10 personal devices. Both include 12 months of Praecheck updates, and your installed version continues to work after that update period.
