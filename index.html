Why this matters

A permission can reach further than it looks.

Modern Android apps are often made from a mixture of first-party code, third-party libraries, system services and device permissions. Looking at one permission at a time can miss how those pieces relate.

01

Embedded components share the app's environment

Analytics, advertising, attribution and other SDKs run inside the host app. Where the app has access to a device capability, embedded code may be technically able to operate within that same environment.

02

Separate signals can become more revealing together

Location, identifiers, network information, app relationships and other signals may each appear limited in isolation. Combined, they can create a more useful basis for profiling, association or identity linkage.

03

Store disclosures cannot show every technical relationship

Privacy labels and policies are useful context, but they are summaries. They do not replace a technical view of what is installed, what access exists, and which embedded components are present on the device.

Important: Praecheck reports technical exposure and capability. It does not claim that a specific app collected, transmitted, sold or shared data unless there is evidence to support that conclusion.

Technical notes

Scope, independent domains and licensing.

What is the assessment scope?

Praecheck evaluates three independent domains: Core exposure from observed/current Android state and granted access; Data correlation exposure from app/SDK capabilities and potential association, profiling or enrichment value; and Known software risks from exact embedded component versions matched against offline security advisories. Findings include evidence context and practical actions where appropriate.

Does the production app transmit assessment data?

No. The production Android app is designed to perform its assessment locally and does not request Android's INTERNET permission. Assessment results and history are stored on the device.

Is Praecheck a malware, spyware or compromise detector?

No. Praecheck is a data exposure assessment tool. It does not claim to detect malware, spyware, surveillance actors or compromise.

What do the three assessment domains mean?

Core exposure prioritises observed/current Android state and granted access. Data correlation exposure separately assesses app/SDK capabilities and the potential usefulness of data combinations for association, profiling or enrichment if the data were actually collected. Known software risks separately identifies exact embedded component versions that match the offline advisory catalogue. The domains are independent and are not evidence that a phone is compromised or that a specific app sold, shared or transmitted data.

Why are repeated assessments useful?

Your exposure profile can change when you install or remove apps, change permissions, update Android, travel, change networks or use the device differently. Praecheck can compare compatible local assessments to show what changed.

How is assessment history retained?

Assessment history, device role and recheck preferences are stored locally on the Android device. Praecheck keeps a bounded history of up to 50 assessments.

How do Personal and Power licences differ?

Both include the same Praecheck features. Personal allows activation on up to 2 personal devices; Power allows activation on up to 10 personal devices. Power does not add business, consultant or resale rights.

How is Praecheck distributed and activated?

Download the signed APK from the official Praecheck download page and install it on Android. A valid signed .pcl licence file is required for full functionality. Direct web purchasing and purchase activation are temporarily unavailable while the payment process is being updated.
